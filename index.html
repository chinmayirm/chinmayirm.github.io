A weathered manuscript of education, craft, and the long arc of building with data.

Essay · MakeMyTrip · 2026

Teaching Autosuggest to Listen

Vague queries are not noise—they are unfinished intent. On MakeMyTrip’s Data Science team I worked large real-user traffic into a GLiNER NER model for entity-aware autosuggest. The endpoint reached a micro F1 of 0.85 and now runs across the platform, especially on the main page.

The practical win was conversational depth: fewer dead-end suggestions, clearer entity spans, and a research paper that captured what the internship proved in production. That work carried me into Travelplex as a Software Engineer focused on AI engineering.

Field Note · ISRO RRSC South · 2025

Crops from Thirteen Bands

At RRSC South I built an end-to-end remote-sensing pipeline for agricultural monitoring—predicting crop distribution from 10m-resolution, 13-band imagery and a curated spectral library, then exposing seasonal maps through an interactive Streamlit dashboard.

Research · CDSAML · 2024

Attention, Entangled

Quantum circuits woven into Vision Transformer attention and feed-forward blocks—implemented with IBM Qiskit and a custom decoder for detection—became a collaboration with an industry mentor and a paper still unfolding.
